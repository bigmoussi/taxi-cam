# 0.7.12 recovery and speed cutoff

During the reported 0.7.11 time-change testing, the live log recorded a second unobserved command list and `unknown_source_state`. Capture/composition counters remained at 4024/2010 while source draws and PFD stamps continued. The native pair still reported active. A lifecycle-only retry therefore did not detect this condition. Separate earlier transient native inspection failures did recover; they do not establish that the later capture stall recovered.

The new monitor checks advancing composed frames and source draws every 250 ms. With a fresh aircraft pose, two active views and unknown capture state persisting for two seconds, it requests retirement of the owned pair. The existing lifecycle controller must confirm cleanup and obtain a fresh validated pose before recreation. Unknown GPU state is never relabelled as a render target, and failed identity guards cannot be bypassed. Retries remain bounded; ten seconds of advancing captures replenish the retry allowance.

The new speed policy inhibits the camera strictly above 60 knots using valid ground-speed telemetry. It latches an OFF request even when button telemetry is briefly unavailable. Once a side is freshly observed ON, its aircraft push event is sent once and accepted transmissions await OFF feedback. A synchronous send failure may retry at most once per second. Falling below the threshold does not silently restore an unacknowledged ON state. Manual diagnostic delivery follows the same speed inhibition.

The button inputs use the public [SimConnect event mapping](https://docs.flightsimulator.com/msfs2024/retail/programming-apis/simconnect/api-reference/events-and-data/simconnect_mapclienteventtosimevent/) and [event transmission](https://docs.flightsimulator.com/msfs2024/retail/programming-apis/simconnect/api-reference/events-and-data/simconnect_transmitclientevent/) interfaces. The current aircraft's own controller consumes the named TAXI push events and publishes its light outputs; the output Lvars are not overwritten.

Focused regressions cover threshold boundaries, invalid/stale/future samples, a cutoff during missing button telemetry, both sides, acknowledgement, duplicate-toggle prevention, send-failure pacing, the observed capture stall and preservation of unknown-state refusal. Recovery has 93 policy/controller checks. Full build/GPU validation and exact-binary ReShade smoke receipts are stored locally under `build/` and archived with the matching release binary.

Live acceptance remains separate: change 01:00 to 11:00 with TAXI ON and confirm completed frames resume without a button toggle, then verify both active TAXI sides clear above 60 knots and stay OFF when speed falls. The user reports improved but still occasional nose-wheel motion artifacts; no new motion correction is claimed for 0.7.12. Missing airport lights remain unresolved.
