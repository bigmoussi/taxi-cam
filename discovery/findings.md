## Backend record to native D3D12 resource

The subsequent saved captures `camera-record-create.json`, `camera-resource-cleanup.json`, `camera-native-resource-and-erase.json` and `camera-d3d12-wrapper.json` under the same `build/msfs-1.8.16.0-` prefix establish a static backend-record-to-resource relationship. The deferred worker **64656624** takes an original record pointer from queue-entry+72, copies its fields into a local record, and calls **64511152** at **64657831** with that local record in R9. The wrapper calls **64511552** at **64511385**, retaining the record as its fourth argument. On success, the worker calls **64750528** at **64657865** to copy the updated record back. Two call sequences independently support identifying wrapper+168 as an **ID3D12Resource pointer**. The before states include `COPY_DEST` (1024), `RENDER_TARGET` (4) and `DEPTH_WRITE` (16). This is stronger evidence than a field name or a pointer-shaped value. The constructor also initializes a state-tracking member at +200, but its update and queue-ordering contract has not been established.

The main creation routine has three observed resource-source paths. An explicitly supplied pointer is queried through resource-vtable+80 (`GetDesc`) and retained through +8 (`AddRef`) before wrapping. A shared-handle path passes record+200 to device-vtable+256, matching `ID3D12Device::OpenSharedHandle`, and then queries the returned resource description. The normal allocation paths call **64620560** or **64621216** and feed their native output into the same wrapper constructor. The optional record+24 wrapper is created separately from a buffer description with readback heap type 3 and initial `COPY_DEST` state; it must not be confused with the primary texture wrapper at +16.

The fully decoded allocation helpers narrow the remaining creation trace to **65008448** and **65008704**.
